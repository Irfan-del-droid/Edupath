# EduPath — 3-Minute Hackathon Demo Script

This script guarantees a high-impact, flawless demonstration for hackathon judges and evaluators.

---

## Storyline & Persona
- **Candidate**: Arjun Mehta, Senior Product Specialist targeting a promotion to **AI Product Manager**.
- **The Problem**: Arjun has completed multiple video courses on AI, but still fails technical interviews because he lacks **verified proof of ability** in AI evaluation frameworks and agent prompt/guardrail architecture.
- **The Solution**: EduPath continuously observes his portfolio, computes DAG skill dependencies, identifies his single highest-leverage gap (**AI Evaluation at 38%**), and prescribes an authentic workplace challenge with real-time rubric feedback.

## 3-Minute Walkthrough Protocol

### Step 1: The Landing Page & Positioning (0:00 – 0:30)
1. Open `http://localhost:5173`.
2. Point out the editorial manifesto: *"Turn career aspirations into verified proof of ability."*
3. Highlight the 4-phase closed loop: **Observe $\to$ Reason $\to$ Act $\to$ Verify & Replan**.
4. Click **"Instant Demo (Arjun)"** or log in with:
   - Email: `arjun@demo.edupath.ai`
   - Password: `demo1234`

### Step 2: The Intelligence Surface (0:30 – 1:15)
1. Land on `/dashboard`. Show the editorial layout:
   - **Career Readiness Score**: 72% (Solid product fundamentals, but blocked on AI evaluation).
   - **Fig. 01 / Skill System**: Visual balance between verified competencies and developing gaps.
   - **Fig. 02 / Largest Verified Gap**: `AI Evaluation Frameworks (38%)` with red/amber priority.
   - **Fig. 04 / Next Best Action**: Instant intervention spotlight: *"Formulate accuracy, hallucination, and safety evaluation rubrics for customer support."*
2. Briefly toggle to `/skills` to show the interactive React Flow DAG graph:
   - Click the **AI Evaluation** node. Notice the inspector drawer showing that prerequisites (`Product Discovery` 86% and `LLM Concepts` 54%) are already met.

### Step 3: Tackling the Challenge & Submitting Proof (1:15 – 2:15)
1. Click **"Tackle Challenge"** directly from the Next Best Action card or navigate to `/challenges`.
2. Inspect Challenge #04:
   - Point out the **Business Scenario** (Enterprise deflection bot with high hallucination risk).
   - Point out the **Rubric**: Structure (Coherence), Depth (Metrics), Practicality (Feasibility).
3. Click **"Submit Proof of Work"** (navigates to `/proof`).
4. Click the button: **"Auto-fill Comprehensive Sample Submission"**.
   - Show how it populates a realistic, professional evaluation framework document with accuracy metrics, hallucination benchmarks, and human-in-the-loop review costs.
5. Click **"Submit for AI Evaluation"**.

### Step 4: The Evaluation Report & Live Agent Replanning (2:15 – 3:00)
1. The submission is evaluated against the 3-dimensional rubric:
   - **Overall Score**: ~84/100
   - **Structure Score**: ~88/100
   - **Depth Score**: ~80/100
   - **Practicality Score**: ~84/100
   - Detailed Strengths, Weaknesses, and Missing Elements.
2. Return to `/dashboard` or `/readiness`:
   - Note that **AI Evaluation** is now upgraded and verified.
   - The **Career Readiness Score** has increased.
   - The **Roadmap** has dynamically adapted to focus on the next sequential frontier (**Agent Architecture & Guardrails**).
3. Conclude: *"EduPath transforms career progression from passive consumption to verifiable capability."*
